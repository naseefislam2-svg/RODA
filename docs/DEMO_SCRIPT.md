# One-minute demo script

**0:00–0:10 — Common ground.** Open Explore. Point out the sample label, public price band, and bidder-first language. “RODA gives independent workers a fair starting line.”

**0:10–0:22 — Private workspace.** Open Solar Commons. Enter an in-range offer and a local vault passphrase. Save it. Show that exact offer, salt, and worker secret remain in the encrypted device vault.

**0:22–0:33 — Disclosure review.** Move across the privacy boundary: the offer remains private; validity, commitment, replay tag, and transaction metadata become public. Explain that the wallet-selected prover is a trusted boundary.

**0:33–0:47 — User-owned deployment.** Connect 1AM on Preview. Deploy the worker contract and approve in the wallet. The app waits for finalization, then shows and retains the actual contract address and deployment transaction hash.

**0:47–0:56 — Real circuit call.** Click “Prove & seal my offer.” Approve the circuit transaction. Show the finalized receipt and explorer link. No fake receipt appears while the call is pending.

**0:56–1:00 — Choice remains.** Explain that the bidder can withdraw before the deadline or explicitly disclose the committed price after it. There is no escrow, payment, or automatic winner selection in this MVP.
