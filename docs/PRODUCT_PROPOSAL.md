# RODA product proposal

## Concepts considered

### RODA — private bidding rooms

- **Pitch:** Independent workers submit a price inside a public band without exposing it to competing bidders.
- **Users:** Freelancers, studios, creative cooperatives, and community commissioners.
- **Problem:** Public price competition rewards undercutting and exposes commercial strategy.
- **Private:** Exact offer, worker secret, and blinding salt.
- **Public:** Commission policy, commitment, replay tag, transaction metadata, and a later voluntary opening.
- **Why Midnight:** The chain enforces a valid price range and deadline while the price remains hidden.
- **Gemini:** Explains the public brief and disclosure model from a fixed public input schema.
- **Direction:** Brazilian Afrofuturist neo-industrial commons: warm mineral materials, circular infrastructure, editorial typography, and signal colors.

### Pulso — anonymous cooperative pulse

- **Pitch:** Cooperative members privately score working conditions while the group sees thresholded trends.
- **Users:** Worker-owned studios and small collectives.
- **Problem:** Small teams can infer who gave critical feedback.
- **Private:** Individual answers and respondent secret.
- **Public:** Minimum-cohort aggregates and survey policy.
- **Why Midnight:** Proofs can count eligible responses without publishing each response.
- **Gemini:** Summarizes approved aggregate results only.
- **Direction:** Civic instrumentation with rhythmic charts and soft technical surfaces.

### Ponte — confidential skills proof

- **Pitch:** Workers prove they meet a public experience threshold without publishing their employment history.
- **Users:** Independent workers and community project organizers.
- **Problem:** Screening asks for more career history than a project needs.
- **Private:** Exact experience values and source credential.
- **Public:** Requirement, issuer, and pass/fail proof.
- **Why Midnight:** Selective proof replaces disclosure of an entire résumé.
- **Gemini:** Turns a public scope into plain-language eligibility criteria.
- **Direction:** Archival dossiers crossed with contemporary public infrastructure.

## Selection

RODA won because privacy changes the market behavior itself. The sealed price is easy to demonstrate in a minute, the public/private boundary is legible, and the workflow gives each bidder a concrete asset: a worker-owned contract deployed from their wallet. The MVP avoids custody and winner-selection claims while still making a real Midnight circuit call.

The product deliberately does not claim one-person-one-bid across separate worker contracts. Its nullifier prevents replay inside the worker contract. Identity or membership uniqueness would require a separate privacy-preserving credential system.
